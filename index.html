02 // pipeline

From claim files to clean data.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    EOBs, 837 claims and self-pay documents arrive in every shape a client can produce.

  2. 02

    Split

    An automated splitting engine breaks huge batches into indexed, traceable pages.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Index-based schema validation catches bad data before anyone downstream sees it.

  4. 04

    Transform

    The EDI engine maps every record into one consistent, HIPAA-compliant model.

  5. 05

    Deliver & sync

    A customisable output engine writes 835, CSV or TXT and syncs results live into the client portal.

edi-engine.log
  1. [ingest] 3 files received (EOB, 837, SELF-PAY)
  2. [split] 3 → 12 pages, indexed
  3. [validate] schema ok ✓ 12/12
  4. [transform] engine → canonical model
  5. [deliver] 835 · csv · txt → portal ✓ synced
